when i was in 9th grade, i played tenor sax second chair
I grew up watching the marching bands play. It was one of my childhood dreams to join once I got to high school. I had played sax since I was in 6th grade, audtioned for the jazz band, was selected for the state-wide honors band in 8th grade.
But I didn't last another year. Each day during marching band was a torture I so desperately tried to mend. Band camp in the summer darkened my skin. No amount of covering could prevent it. My hair grew nappier with the sun and sweat。 And I mean literally grew. I had to wear 2 different sized hats depending on how recently straightened my hair had been or if it was a wash day. My pale peers took notice. Every day I was reminded I was inferior. Every. Damn. Day. I haven't played the sax since.
